# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Project
## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze student performance data
and identify important patterns, trends, correlations, and influencing factors.
## Dataset
The dataset contains information about:
- Student ID
- Gender
- Study Hours
- Attendance
- Mathematics Score
- Physics Score
- Computer Science Score
## Analysis Performed
### 1. Statistical Summary
Basic statistical measures such as:
- Mean
- Median
- Minimum
- Maximum
- Standard deviation
were analyzed using Pandas.
### 2. Missing Values
The dataset was checked for missing values to understand
the quality of the data.
### 3. Study Hours Analysis
A histogram was created to understand the distribution
of study hours among students.
### 4. Subject Performance
Average scores in Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science
were compared using a bar chart.
### 5. Study Hours and Performance
A scatter plot was used to study the relationship between
study hours and Mathematics scores.
### 6. Attendance and Performance
Attendance was compared with the students' average scores
to identify whether attendance influences performance.
### 7. Correlation Analysis
A correlation matrix and heatmap were used to identify
relationships between numerical variables.
### 8. Top Performing Students
The top 10 students were identified based on their average score.
### 9. Gender-wise Analysis
Average subject performance was compared between male and female students.
## Key Findings
- Study hours generally have a positive relationship with student performance.
- Higher attendance is generally associated with better scores.
- Correlation analysis helps identify important relationships between variables.
- The average score helps identify high-performing students.
- Visualization makes patterns and trends easier to understand.
## Conclusion
The EDA process helped identify important patterns in the student
performance dataset. Statistical summaries and visualizations
provided a better understanding of the factors associated with
academic performance.
## Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
